## 2. Preserved Historical Audit Findings (Pre-Phase I Bugs Fixed & Root Causes)

To prevent regression or amnesia when superseded docs move to `reports/historical/`, all unique root-cause findings from earlier audits remain anchored here:

1. **76.0s Hardcoded Baseline & Fake Constants (Fixed pre-Phase H):** Early vehicle/tyre logic initialized every driver at `76.0s` (Monaco bias) and emitted `+0.12/+0.08/+0.04` fake tyre degradation slopes and invented `stint_age=23` values when data was absent. Replaced cleanly by `RollingCleanAirPaceEstimator` and `FuelCompensatedDegradationModel`.
2. **Permissive Clean-Air Default & Standing-Restart Averaging (Fixed Phase I `a130ef7`, `5afd7b9`):** `VehicleResponseAgent` and `TyreStrategyAgent` treated missing `interval` as clean air, and `RollingCleanAirPaceEstimator` averaged `len==1` standing-restart laps (`160.1s` in Silverstone Lap 4 after Red Flag) with Lap 5 flying pace (`92.8s`), causing a bogus `126.47s` median and `33.5s` residual spikes that also corrupted unguarded `_advance_track_evo`. Fixed cleanly via tri-state `CleanAirStatus`, `<= 5.0s` evo jump guard, and immediate flying-lap recovery.
3. **Agent 05 Missing Field Name & Silent Promotion (Fixed Phase I `a130ef7`):** `ConsequenceFusionEngine.on_finding()` read `result.get("verdict")` instead of `IntegrityAgent`'s `result["integrity_state"]`, missing SIA `DEGRADED`/`UNKNOWN` states, while `_publish_superseding_v2()` promoted uncalibrated `PRIOR_UNMEASURED` findings when no blocking alert arrived. Fixed strictly using normalized SIA state parsing (`integrity_state` + `finality_status`) and positive corroboration rules.
4. **Flink Normalizer v2 Driver-State Drop & Cold `tracemalloc` Distortion (Fixed Phase H -> Profiled Phase I):** Earlier Flink SQL omitted high-frequency `driver_state` normalization (fixed in `normalizer_job.sql` v3), and Phase H's `83.8-87.5s` cold seek measurement included Python `tracemalloc` profiling overhead alongside unindexed `Ledger.facts` scans (`7.7M` `scope_in` calls) and `model_copy(deep=True)` (`4.28M` deepcopies). Removing `tracemalloc` + selective Phase I optimizations cut cold seek to `14.69s` uncontended (`-30.3%` below the `21.08s` clean pre-opt baseline).
5. **FastF1 Local Cache vs Stubbed 2023/2024 Directories & Phase J OpenF1 Expansion:** `data/fastf1_cache/` contains 6 complete offline-loadable Grand Prix races (`2021` British, Belgian, Saudi Arabian, Abu Dhabi; `2022` Monaco, Japanese). The 4 `2023/2024` corpus directories in `data/f1_corpus/` hold Jolpica summary metadata without lap-by-lap timestamps (`valid_lt = 0`) and remain excluded from lap modeling. In Phase J (`31a323b`), `11` complete `2023–2024` Grands Prix were cached cleanly from the public OpenF1 REST archive into `data/openf1_cache/` to validate unseen external generalization (`17` total races across 4 seasons).
6. **Phase J Benchmark Reconciliations (`c28c88f`, `232f9a3`):** Empirical boundary decomposition resolved the Phase I `14.69s` vs `70.88s` replay gap (showing `89.04%` SIA attribution in uncontended `14.98s` seeks vs `32–81s` CPU/thermal throttling under contended subprocess runs) and separated Tier 1 raw broker sweeps (`73.7k–83.8k msg/s`) from Tier 2 complete Flink v3 + 6-Agent + unpaused SIA WAL + per-event SQLite sink E2E throughput (`9.67 ev/s` over `110.86s`).
